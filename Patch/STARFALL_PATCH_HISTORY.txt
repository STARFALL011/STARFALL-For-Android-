STARFALL: INFINITE ASSAULT
CONSOLIDATED PATCH HISTORY
========================================

LATEST PATCH
- Loading an existing saved account now returns to the main menu (home screen)
  instead of jumping straight into the lobby. The player can then press START
  to enter the lobby and use PLAY/DEPLOY as intended.

MENU AND ACCOUNT SYSTEM
- Main menu flow: START, ACCOUNT, EXIT.
- Account screen supports selecting/loading saved accounts, creating a new account,
  deleting a selected account with confirmation, and activating Admin Tester.
- New normal accounts start with 300 coins.
- Account cards display each saved account's name and current coin balance.
- Admin Tester is labeled ADMIN rather than showing a normal player's balance.
- Selected account is visually highlighted.
- Account name input is hidden until CREATE NEW ACCOUNT is pressed.
- Account progress is stored locally on the device/browser. This is not online
  authentication or cloud synchronization.
- Achievement progress and reward claims are intended to be stored per account.

LOBBY AND NAVIGATION
- Lobby displays the selected ship, rarity, ship skill label, and coin balance.
- Removed the duplicate coin display beneath the featured ship.
- Settings shortcut belongs in the lobby; gameplay should keep the pause control
  as the primary top-bar action.
- Ship Hangar, Flight Manual, and Achievements cards were compacted for a
  single-row mobile layout.
- Pause menu is the place to access resume/exit-run actions.

SHIP HANGAR AND SHIPS
- Hangar is designed to show up to six ships per page with left/right navigation.
- Ship details include name, life/health, damage, speed, and special skill.
- Ship upgrade progress is intended to remain specific to each ship.
- Existing ship artwork/assets are retained where referenced by the project.
- The project includes the Space 5 / Galaxy Drift background asset from the
  supplied assets archive.

GAMEPLAY AND CONTROLS
- Drag movement is the primary ship movement input; a simple tap should not
  move the ship.
- Double-tap is used for teleport with temporary immunity, subject to the
  implemented cooldown and gameplay state.
- The in-game pause control is used to pause and access run options.
- Early enemy waves were requested to use gentler, less dense firing patterns,
  with difficulty increasing gradually in later waves.
- Skill handling was separated by skill/ship to reduce effects accidentally
  carrying over from a previously activated skill.
- Epic and higher-rarity skill buttons were requested to have stronger rarity-
  themed visual effects and clearer activation/cooldown feedback.
- Omega ships were requested to have a permanently active aura beneath the ship
  and three unique Omega skills.

BACKGROUND AND AUDIO
- Space 5 / Galaxy Drift was added to the background selection.
- Space backgrounds use a slight zoom and gentle automatic drift for a moving
  background effect.
- Audio controls are available from the lobby; gameplay audio access should be
  managed through the pause/settings flow as implemented.

ACHIEVEMENTS AND ECONOMY
- Achievement rewards grant coins when first unlocked.
- Rewards should not be claimable repeatedly for the same account/achievement.
- Each player account should retain its own achievement progress and rewards.
- Existing accounts retain their own saved coin balances; only new normal
  accounts receive the 300-coin starting balance.

PROJECT NOTES
- The project is an offline-friendly HTML/CSS/JavaScript game intended for
  SPCK Editor mobile preview.
- Account data uses browser localStorage on the current device/browser.
- This document consolidates the previous patch-note files. It is a record of
  requested/implemented project changes, not a guarantee that every feature has
  been exhaustively tested on a physical Android device.
- Recommended final checks in SPCK Preview:
  1. Load a normal account and confirm it returns to the main menu.
  2. Press START and enter the lobby.
  3. Verify account coins, Hangar, skills, pause menu, and per-account achievements.
  4. Test on the target phone for responsive layout and touch controls.

POWER-UP VISUALS & EFFECTS UPDATE
- Coin Magnet now instantly collects all coin pickups currently inside the playfield; other boosts remain.
- Health restores exactly one heart, respecting the ship's maximum health.
- Shield lasts 4 seconds and displays a vertical countdown meter on the left side of the playfield.
- Speed Boost doubles the selected ship's base movement speed (+100%) for 5 seconds, then restores the exact ship-specific speed.
- Rapid Fire increases the normal fire rate by 50% for 6 seconds, respecting the game's fire-rate cap.
- Ultimate Power-Up creates a glowing yellow laser that sweeps from the bottom toward the top, clearing regular enemies and enemy projectiles while preserving collectible power-ups. Bosses are destroyed and award their normal bonus.
- Power-up pickups now have stronger, pulsing glow and distinct icons/shapes.
- Flight Manual documents each power-up's visual behavior and exact effect.


PATCH REVISION — SKILL & POWER-UP BALANCE
- Phoenix Rebirth now restores full 3-heart HP, retains the blue-flame rapid-fire effect, and has a 30-second cooldown.
- Void Freeze chains a visible energy strike across up to 3 nearby enemies and briefly freezes each hit target; cooldown remains 17 seconds.
- Helix Beam cooldown is now 8 seconds.
- Polar Field launches 8 fast homing rockets; cooldown is 14 seconds.
- Grim Barrage's rift is deployed well away from the player (approximately 40% of the playfield width/height).
- Celestial Rain now displays its remaining duration.
- Event Horizon's black hole follows in front of the player, offset by 20% of the playfield height.
- Omega Protocol was renamed Aetherion Cataclysm.
- Crimson Singularity is thrown approximately 50% of the canvas away, pulls enemies for 3 seconds with a duration indicator, then clears enemies and hostile projectiles across the canvas.
- Bloodline Erasure now follows the player's movement for 6 seconds and shows its remaining duration.
- Red Dominion now summons 5 rotating escorts that fire homing rockets once per second for 15 seconds; cooldown is 30 seconds.
- Rapid Fire power-up now doubles normal firing rate (+100%) for 6 seconds, subject to the game's global fire-rate cap.
- Coin Magnet now lasts 12 seconds, attracts all coin pickups across the canvas, and shows a gold vertical countdown meter.
- Flight Manual updated with the revised power-up and high-tier skill behavior.


PATCH UPDATE — GRIM BARRAGE, CRIMSON SINGULARITY & SPACE COLORS
- Grim Barrage now spawns directly in front of the player at 40% of the playfield height and follows the ship, rather than appearing to the left or right.
- Crimson Singularity now stays 20% of the playfield height in front of the moving player during its 3-second pull, then triggers a large expanding red explosion wave that clears enemies and hostile projectiles while preserving power-ups.
- Space background labels and fallback palettes now match: Space 1 Pale Blue, Space 2 Violet, Space 3 Blue Galaxy, Space 4 Red, Space 5 Pink Galaxy. Subtle color tinting helps each theme read clearly while retaining the original image assets.

- Added a red glowing dot to the Lobby Achievements card whenever at least one reward is ready to claim.
- Claimed achievements remain saved and cannot be claimed a second time.

[2026-10-16] RANK SYSTEM
- Added persistent Rank Points (RP) to each normal player account.
- Earn RP after each run: 1 RP per enemy kill, 10 RP per round reached, and 50 bonus RP per defeated boss.
- Rank thresholds: Cadet (0), Pilot (500), Ace (1,500), Elite (3,500), Galactic Commander (7,000).
- Run results show RP earned and the player's current rank; lobby shows current rank and progress to the next rank.
- Account selection cards show each normal player's rank and RP before loading the account. Admin Tester is explicitly excluded from rank progression and rank display.
- Ship purchase requirements by rarity: Common=Cadet, Normal=Pilot, Special=Ace, Epic=Elite, Legendary/Omega=Galactic Commander. Rank unlocks purchase eligibility; coins are still required.
- Rank points are saved per account and persist between runs.


RANK TITLES AND ACHIEVEMENT EXPANSION
- Added premium rank title styling with distinct Cadet, Pilot, Ace, Elite, and Galactic Commander colors. Higher ranks receive brighter glow/shimmer effects; Commander gets a gold cosmic aura.
- Account selection rank labels use the matching rank color. Admin Tester remains excluded from rank progression.
- Expanded achievements into three visible groups: Warrior, Collector, and Rank.
- Added additional warrior milestones, collectible milestones, and rank-promotion achievements.
- Achievement rewards remain manual claims; the red notification dot indicates rewards waiting to be claimed.
- Rank achievements are checked after the run's RP is applied so promotions can unlock achievements immediately.
