POWER-UPS

◈ COIN MAGNET — Activates for 12 seconds, attracts all coin pickups anywhere in the canvas to the player, and shows a gold vertical duration meter on the left. Other boosts are not collected.

✚ HEALTH — Restores exactly 1 heart, up to the ship's maximum health.

⬡ SHIELD — Protects the ship from damage for 4 seconds. A vertical shield timer appears along the left side of the playfield and counts down.

» SPEED BOOST — Doubles the selected ship's current movement speed (+100%) for 5 seconds.

✦ RAPID FIRE — Doubles the ship's normal firing rate (+100%) for 6 seconds, respecting the game's fire-rate cap.

✹ ULTIMATE LASER — Fires a bright, glowing yellow screen-clearing laser from the bottom toward the top. It clears all regular enemies and enemy projectiles on screen; collectible power-ups remain untouched. Bosses are also destroyed for their normal bonus reward.

EPIC, LEGENDARY & OMEGA SKILLS

❄ VOID FREEZE — Freezes enemies and chains through up to 3 nearby enemies with a bouncing energy strike. Cooldown: 17 seconds.

✧ HELIX BEAM — Piercing beam skill. Cooldown: 8 seconds.

🚀 POLAR FIELD — Launches 8 fast homing rockets. Cooldown: 14 seconds.

🕳 GRIM BARRAGE — Deploys a rift directly in front of the player, 40% of the playfield height away, and follows the ship while pulling enemies inward.

☄ CELESTIAL RAIN — Calls down a multi-lane beam strike with a visible remaining-duration indicator.

◉ EVENT HORIZON — Keeps its black hole in front of the ship at a distance of 20% of the playfield height, following the player.

✦ AETHERION CATACLYSM — Formerly Omega Protocol; activates the Omega ship's multi-lane beam, shield, rapid fire, and aura.

🔥 PHOENIX REBIRTH — Restores full 3-heart HP and triggers blue-flame rapid fire. Cooldown: 30 seconds.

☄ CRIMSON SINGULARITY — Creates a singularity 20% of the playfield height in front of the player and follows while pulling for 3 seconds. It then detonates in a large red explosion wave, clearing enemies and hostile projectiles across the canvas while leaving power-ups untouched.

⚡ BLOODLINE ERASURE — A crimson laser follows the ship for 6 seconds; the remaining duration is shown on screen.

🚀 RED DOMINION — Summons 5 rotating escort ships that fire homing rockets once per second for 15 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds.
